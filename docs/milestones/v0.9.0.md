## Overview

Milestone 9 (`v0.9.0`) delivers **Supplier SCAR & Vendor Rating** for the **Engine-Powered Quality Engineering Skills** platform: `quality_core.sqe` — a deterministic supplier performance and corrective-action engine comprising a PPM calculator, an OTIF calculator, a weighted vendor scorecard, a threshold-triggered escalation ladder, and a SCAR generator whose linked evidence is validated by the engines that own it.

It is the platform's **fourth and final net-new domain** (after the RCA Suite at `v0.6.0`, the NCR/COPQ engines at `v0.7.0`, and PPAP Core at `v0.8.0`), and the last domain milestone before `v1.0.0` Production Hardening.

Per [`ROADMAP.md`](../../ROADMAP.md), this slot was chosen over the full ISO 9001/IATF clause-audit engine and VDA 6.3 precisely because it is "mostly business-metric arithmetic (PPM/OTIF formulas), not a large standards-citation surface." Scoping confirmed that reading — and surfaced its consequence, which shapes the entire milestone: **when there is no standard, the engine must say so, loudly, at every layer.**

### What the SQE engine does

1. **Supplier PPM** — defective parts per million over a caller-defined evaluation period, reported with its numerator, denominator, and lot count, and a sample-adequacy annotation.
2. **OTIF** — on-time, in-full, and the composite conjunction, under caller-stated window and tolerance rules rather than baked-in ones.
3. **Vendor scorecard** — a weighted composite across quality, delivery, and optionally cost, resolved to an A/B/C band, with every dimension's raw metric, sub-score, weight, and contribution visible.
4. **Threshold-triggered escalation** — a tier ladder (`NONE` → `MONITOR` → `SCAR_REQUIRED` → `CONTAINMENT_REQUIRED` → `EXECUTIVE_REVIEW`) whose *structure* is cited to AIAG CQI-20 and whose *numeric triggers* are declared heuristics, reported with the full evaluated trigger set.
5. **SCAR generation** — a corrective-action request structured per AIAG CQI-20 and the Ford Global 8D Manual, which **requests** a root cause and validates a supplier-returned one through `quality_core.rca` rather than authoring one.

### 🔒 The no-standard-implied invariant

ISO 9001:2015 §8.4 and IATF 16949:2016 §8.4 require that suppliers be **evaluated and monitored against criteria**. Neither supplies the criteria. There is **no published standard** anywhere for PPM acceptance thresholds, OTIF windows, scorecard weights, A/B/C band boundaries, or escalation trigger levels.

No engine, canvas, MCP tool payload, MCP tool description, or skill body delivered in this milestone may present any of those numbers as a standards requirement. Every one is **caller-configurable**, carries a **documented default**, and is **labelled an engineering heuristic in the payload**. Clause citations name ISO §8.4 / IATF §8.4 only as *the requirement to evaluate*, never as the source of a number.

This invariant is enforced at four layers (engine, canvas, tool payload and description, skill body) by mandatory negative controls, and is the single most important correctness constraint in Milestone 9. It is the direct analogue of Milestone 8's authority invariant: a tool that fabricates a standard is not making a wording mistake, it is asserting an authority it does not have.

Two companion invariants, enforced the same way:

- **🔒 Commercial authority** — new-business hold, de-sourcing, resourcing, and charge-back are business decisions. The escalation engine recommends a *tier* and shows its evidence; it never emits a commercial action as its own verdict.
- **🔒 Root-cause authorship** — the supplier owns the root cause. The SCAR generator requests one and validates one; it never authors, infers, or paraphrases one. A SCAR generator that writes the root cause has inverted the point of corrective action.

### Cross-engine linkage — the Milestone 8 pattern, repeated

Milestone 8's retrospective asked explicitly whether the [E7] #105 verdict-affecting linkage pattern should be repeated in `v0.9.0`. It is, in [E6] #120:

| SQE input | Owning engine | Shipped |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Linked nonconformity / disposition evidence feeding a SCAR | `quality_core.ncr` | `v0.7.0` |
| Supplier-returned root cause (reversible 5-Why, Is/Is-Not scoping) | `quality_core.rca` | `v0.6.0` |
| Cost impact on the SCAR and the scorecard | `quality_core.copq` | `v0.7.0` |

Linkage is **verdict-affecting**: a linked NCR that fails `quality_core.ncr` validation, or a supplier root cause the `quality_core.rca` reversible 5-Why validator rejects as circular or superficial, resolves `EVIDENCE_INVALID` — and an `EVIDENCE_INVALID` SCAR is not issuable and never closable. A SCAR cannot be closed on a root cause that RCA would reject.

### Product Triad architecture

- **Deterministic Core** (`packages/quality-core/src/quality_core/sqe/`): typed Pydantic schemas, the PPM and OTIF calculators, the scorecard, the escalation ladder, the SCAR generator and its cross-engine linkage, `ASSUMPTIONS_LOG.md`, and a machine-checkable `CITATIONS.tsv` validated against the on-machine ISO/IATF excerpts and the CQI-20 / Ford 8D manuals.
- **Visual Single-Writer Canvas** (`packages/quality-core/src/quality_core/canvas/sqe.py`): `SQECanvas` rendering the supplier × dimension scorecard matrix in dark/light HTML with a benchmark dataset, heuristic captions intact.
- **FastMCP Tool Layer** (`packages/quality-mcp/src/quality_mcp/tools/sqe.py`): exposes `calculate_supplier_ppm`, `calculate_otif`, `calculate_vendor_scorecard`, `evaluate_escalation`, `generate_scar`, and `render_sqe_canvas`.
- **AI Agent Skill** (`skills/supplier-scar/`): `agentskills.io` compliant, enforcing Zero Inline Math / Zero Inline Adjudication — never computes a metric inline, never assigns a band from memory, never presents a heuristic as a standard, never authors a supplier's root cause.
- **Chained Client Round-Trip**: an in-process JSON-RPC integration suite validating three-way payload parity and a chained **NCR → SCAR** workflow across a single FastMCP client session, in both its valid and invalid halves.

1. **Workspace Sync & Dependency Lockdown**: `uv sync --frozen` executes cleanly across all workspace members.
2. **Headless Dependency Isolation**: `quality_core.sqe` pulls no app/UI/Streamlit-chain dependency; the `quality-mcp` export confirms zero UI-chain resolution.
3. **Static Analysis & Type Integrity**: `uv run ruff check .`, `uv run mypy`, and `uv run pip-audit` report zero errors across all packages and tests.
4. **100% Line & Branch Coverage Enforcement**: the core gate achieves 100% line and branch coverage including `--cov=quality_core.sqe`; the `quality-mcp` gate remains at 100%; the governance suites pass.
5. **Reference-Material Gate**: `ISO_9001_2015_Section_8_4_and_10_2.md` and `IATF_16949_2016_Section_8_4.md` are on-machine with every listed clause verified present by search, and the AIAG CQI-20 / Ford Global 8D paths are recorded. No SQE constant is asserted from model recall or from a web search.
6. **Standards Fidelity of the SQE Domain**: `quality_core.sqe` carries `ASSUMPTIONS_LOG.md` and `CITATIONS.tsv`; every citation traces to an on-machine source, verified with formatting-tolerant matching. Every SCAR section heading is traceable to CQI-20 or the Ford Global 8D Manual.
7. **🔒 No-Standard-Implied Gate (four layers)**: no engine result, canvas rendering, MCP tool payload or description, or skill body presents a weight, band boundary, PPM threshold, OTIF window, or escalation trigger as a standards requirement. Every one is caller-configurable and labelled an engineering heuristic. Proven load-bearing at every layer by mutation negative controls.
8. **🔒 Commercial-Authority Gate**: no layer presents a new-business hold, de-sourcing, resourcing, or charge-back as a platform recommendation. Mutation-proven.
9. **🔒 Root-Cause-Authorship Gate**: no layer authors, infers, or paraphrases a supplier's root cause; a SCAR with no supplier response never reaches `CLOSABLE`, and a validated root cause without a stated verification-of-effectiveness does not close it either. Mutation-proven.
10. **Indeterminacy Gate**: a zero-denominator period resolves `INDETERMINATE` end-to-end — engine, canvas, tool, and skill — and never `0.0` PPM; an `INDETERMINATE` metric suppresses the scorecard band; an `INDETERMINATE` scorecard yields neither escalation nor clearance. ## Retrospective & Status

**Status: Not Started — scoped.** Milestone 9 is fully scoped with 12 Epic issues (#114–#125) open under [GitHub milestone #10](https://github.com/Siddardth7/quality-engineering-skills/milestone/10). Execution follows the branch ladder `feature → test → main`, one PR per epic into `test`, in dependency order:

```
E0 → E1 → { E2 ∥ E3 } → E4 → { E5 ∥ E6 ∥ E7 } → E8 → E9 → E10 → E11
```

### Scoping decisions recorded

1. **The no-standard-implied invariant is this milestone's headline constraint.** `ROADMAP.md` selected this slot for its small citation surface. The consequence is that most of the numbers here have *no* standard behind them at all — and the risk shifts from mis-citing a standard to inventing one. Every weight, band, threshold, and window is caller-configurable, defaulted, and labelled a declared engineering heuristic at four layers, mutation-proven at each. It is the structural analogue of Milestone 8's authority invariant.
2. **The citable base is split, and E0 is a real procurement.** AIAG CQI-20 and the Ford Global 8D Manual were already converted at `v0.6.0` and cover the corrective-action half at no new cost. ISO 9001 §8.4/§10.2 and IATF 16949 §8.4 are not on-machine, and the full PDFs remain scanned images with no text layer — the same limitation Milestone 8 recorded. They must be hand-produced as clause excerpts, mirroring the `v0.7.0` §8.7 pattern. E1–E6 stay blocked until both are verified present by search.
3. **Cross-engine linkage repeats, and is verdict-affecting.** Milestone 8's retrospective asked whether the [E7] #105 pattern should carry into `v0.9.0`. [E6] #120 answers yes: SCAR evidence is validated by `quality_core.ncr`, `quality_core.rca`, and `quality_core.copq`, and evidence that fails its owning engine blocks issuance. A SCAR cannot close on a root cause the RCA validator would reject.
4. **Two companion authority invariants.** Commercial actions (hold, de-source, charge-back) belong to the business, not to an escalation engine. Root causes belong to the supplier, not to a SCAR generator. Both are enforced by mandatory mutation negative controls at every layer, exactly as the primary invariant is.
5. **Granularity matches `v0.8.0`** — 12 issues, one deliverable each. No issue bundles engine + canvas + tools + skill, so each PR carries one review focus, and E2 ∥ E3 and E5 ∥ E6 ∥ E7 can proceed in parallel.
