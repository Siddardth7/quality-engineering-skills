## Overview

Milestone 11 delivers the 8D Problem-Solving State Machine — the first item promoted out of the named v2 backlog in [`ROADMAP.md`](../../ROADMAP.md), and the highest-complexity domain in `Idea.md`. E0–E13 are merged into `test`. The platform now carries 12 domain skills across nine domains and 34 registered MCP tools.

The delivered surface is `quality_core.rca.eight_d_schema` (the D0–D8 report model), `quality_core.rca.eight_d` (the state machine and its three milestone gates), `quality_core.rca.eight_d_disciplines` (the nine per-discipline validators and the `validate_8d` whole-report sweep), `quality_core.canvas.eight_d` (the single-writer HTML canvas), `quality_mcp.tools.eight_d` (`validate_8d`, `advance_8d`, `render_8d_canvas`), and `skills/8d-problem-solving/SKILL.md`. The Ford Global 8D Manual is primary for every D-label and AIAG CQI-20 backs the problem-solving method; E0 landed 28 `rca/CITATIONS.tsv` rows and 13 `RULE-8D-*` assumptions-log entries with **no** procurement gap, since both manuals are on-box.

Two invariants carried through every layer. First, **the two `closeable` flags are never collapsed onto one name**: `result["closeable"]` is the whole-report gate including the D3→D4 linked-NCR rule `PDD-8D-008`, and `result["d8"]["closeable"]` is closure evidence only. Second, **root causes belong to the analyst, not to the engine** — D4 validates a supplied causal chain through `quality_core.rca.five_why` and never authors, infers, ranks or paraphrases one.

This is an implementation-complete readiness record. Promotion from `test` to `main` and creation/push of the `v1.1.0` tag remain human-owner actions. No `v1.1.0` tag or GitHub Release is claimed. `v1.0.0` likewise remains implementation-complete and unpromoted; this milestone does not change that.

## Release Gate Criteria

The delivered tool-and-skill surface is `validate_8d`, `advance_8d`, and `render_8d_canvas` behind the `8d-problem-solving` skill, composed with the already-shipped `write_ncr`, `validate_5why`, `validate_control_plan`, and `lookup_fmea_ap`. Standards evidence is the on-box Ford Global 8D Manual (primary for every D-label) and AIAG CQI-20 (2nd Edition, 2018).

1. `uv sync --frozen` and the headless dependency contracts pass for both workspace packages.
2. Ruff, mypy, and the dependency vulnerability audit pass.
3. The combined CI core gate covers `quality_core.rca.eight_d`, `eight_d_disciplines`, `eight_d_schema`, and `canvas.eight_d` at 100% line and branch coverage; `quality_mcp` independently remains at 100% with `tools/eight_d.py` included.
4. Every `RULE-8D-*` id is backed by a `rca/CITATIONS.tsv` row quoting an on-box manual, and every gate-enforcement mechanism that no manual prescribes is recorded as a Process Design Decision (`PDD-8D-*`) in `rca/ASSUMPTIONS_LOG.md` rather than presented as a standard. E0 recorded PROCUREMENT-GAP status **none**.
5. Each of the three gates is proven by a negative control asserting the exact `code` *and* `rule_id` *and* that the state did not move — `RULE-8D-GATE-CONTAINMENT` (D3→D4), `RULE-8D-GATE-PREVENTION` (D7→D8), `RULE-8D-GATE-CLOSURE` (D8→CLOSED).
6. The whole-report gate and the closure-evidence flag stay at distinct JSON paths at every layer: `result["closeable"]` versus `result["d8"]["closeable"]`, never collapsed onto one name in a payload, a canvas badge, or the skill body.
7. `packages/quality-mcp/tests/test_eight_d_chained_roundtrip.py` drives NCR → D3 → D4 → D7 → D8 → CLOSED through the real gates in one in-process FastMCP session, with four-leg payload parity on every call.
8. Every `RULE-8D-*` id named in `skills/8d-problem-solving/SKILL.md` exists in `rca/CITATIONS.tsv`, guarded by a governance test with both anti-vacuity directions covered.
9. All workspace version SSOTs and lock entries agree on `1.1.0`; the changelog is dated 2026-09-08.
10. Promotion `test → main` and the `v1.1.0` tag are performed by the human owner after this closeout PR merges.

## Retrospective & Status

**Status: Complete · ready for human release. Completed 2026-09-08.**

E0–E13 merged into `test`; issues #204–#218 remain open pending the human promotion to `main`. The milestone shipped the state machine, the nine disciplines, the canvas, the tool layer, the skill, and the chained integration suite without weakening either coverage gate, and without adding a standards claim the on-box manuals do not carry.

### Key Achievements

1. **A defect class the pipeline is structurally blind to: a report that says the right thing while doing the wrong thing.** E12's `/8d` skill described the two `closeable` flags correctly and then, in Step 3, unconditionally instructed the agent to advance to the adjacent state. At D8 that target is `CLOSED`. Darryl's human review on [PR #236](https://github.com/Siddardth7/quality-engineering-skills/pull/236) named the consequence exactly: *"The resulting workflow can therefore turn a report that `validate_8d` says is not closeable into a `CLOSED` report."* It was reproduced end to end against the live tools before anything was touched — `validate_8d` → `closeable: False` (`LINKED_NCR_INVALID` / `PDD-8D-008`) with `d8.closeable: True`; `advance_8d("CLOSED")` → `ADVANCED`, `state: CLOSED`, `reasons: []`; `validate_8d` again → `state: CLOSED`, `closeable: still False`, reason still present. The engine was correct throughout: `PDD-8D-008` gates D3→D4, not D8→CLOSED. The defect was entirely in the prose — *"a disclaimer about what to **say**, where a precondition on what to **do** was required."* Fixed at `c5262e0`, which put a named closure precondition at the head of Step 3, guarded by a three-mutation governance control whose third mutation restores the exact original defect (a reporting-only prohibition) and must fail.

   **Why this is the milestone's headline lesson: three automated stages held it and none of them caught it.** The coder chose a prose invariant over a worked example, the tester ruled that choice correct, and the round-1 reviewer concurred. All three reasoned carefully about what the skill would *model*; not one asked what the workflow would *do*. Consensus across three stages is not evidence when all three reason from the same frame, and the read-only reviewer restriction does not fix that — it guarantees independence of *authorship*, not of *framing*. A human reading the document as a workflow broke it. The mitigation that generalizes is the one that shipped: for any prose deliverable, state the acceptance criterion as a precondition on an action, then make a negative control restore the original defect rather than merely delete a string.

2. **The same defect class recurred in E13, one epic later, and shipped unresolved.** The Team Lead's review on [PR #237](https://github.com/Siddardth7/quality-engineering-skills/pull/237) recorded finding **F1**: the valid chain asserts `verdict == "ADVANCED"`, `state == "CLOSED"`, `reasons == []`, and `report["status"] == "CLOSED"` — *"It never asserts what `validate_8d` says about the finished report."* The gap was proven live, not theorised: injecting an invalid linked NCR into the carried report immediately before the CLOSED hop left `test_valid_chain_ncr_to_d3_to_d4_to_d7_to_closed` **passing**. The review named the kinship explicitly — *"the same defect class as Darryl's [P1] — asserting that the transition succeeded rather than that the end state is correct"* — and left it non-blocking (*"Your call whether it lands here or as a follow-up"*).

   **Closeout verification, and it is not the comfortable answer: F1 did not land, and at merge time no follow-up issue had been filed.** The merged `packages/quality-mcp/tests/test_eight_d_chained_roundtrip.py` at `9799219` contains no `validate_8d` call on the closed report. The reviewer had written *"say so and I'll open the issue"* and then did not — so for the length of the closeout the defect was diagnosed, accepted as non-blocking, and tracked nowhere. It is now [#238](https://github.com/Siddardth7/quality-engineering-skills/issues/238), filed during this epic once the coder stage surfaced the omission. The suite whose job is end-to-end truth would still green-light a chain producing the outcome E12's fix exists to prevent. Two lines close it. That it recurred immediately after being diagnosed, in an epic written by a different agent that had read the diagnosis, is the finding: naming a defect class in a review does not inoculate the next deliverable against it. Only a check does.

3. **Coverage is not correctness, demonstrated twice inside one epic.** E10 ([PR #234](https://github.com/Siddardth7/quality-engineering-skills/pull/234)) added `citation_basis` to every finding so the renderer reads each finding's standards basis instead of inferring it. **T1:** the field was entirely unguarded. Flipping `ERA_VERIFIED_WITHOUT_IMPLEMENTATION` from `PLATFORM_UNCITED` to `RULE` — presenting a platform heuristic as a Ford requirement, the precise fabrication the field exists to prevent — left **all 298 tests green**, and *"100% branch coverage was fully satisfied the entire time. Coverage proves the line ran, not that the value is right."* The nine pre-existing `citation_basis` entries in tests pinned the key, never the value. **T2:** the fix for T1 had its own hole, found by the read-only reviewer. The new guard parsed only `code="LITERAL"` construction sites, leaving 11 non-literal sites invisible; the reviewer flipped the D8 closure-deficiency comprehension (`code=deficiency.code`) `PDD → RULE` and watched all 300 tests pass. That is the worse failure, because *"a guard that looks like it covers something gets trusted."* The parser now captures the `code` expression verbatim and a third guard pins per-basis site counts and the exact non-literal site map: **64 construction sites — 21 `RULE`, 34 `PDD`, 9 `PLATFORM_UNCITED`**. Those figures are themselves a small third instance of the theme: the first count reported was "53 sites: 18/34/9", and the reviewer caught the miscount and had it corrected in place rather than quietly rewritten.

4. **Four subagent runs died on session rate limits across E10–E12 (tester twice, reviewer twice), and the cost was build/verify independence.** E10's tester died mid-run and the Team Lead completed Stage 3 by hand. E11's tester died *"for the second consecutive epic, at the same point — after writing its suite, before mutation testing"*, and that PR asked in writing for what this section is: *"the tester stage now needs a rate-limit-resilient plan."* E12's reviewer died before reading a single artifact. The response there is the one worth keeping: the run was retried with an incremental-write, priority-ordered brief and **no PR was opened until a real verdict existed** — *"the read-only restriction is the gate, and a self-reviewed pipeline is theatre."* On the two epics where the Team Lead completed the tester stage by hand, the read-only reviewer was explicitly briefed to audit the test stage as hard as the implementation, and it earned that brief: E10's T2 is the reviewer's find, against the Team Lead's own guard. The mitigation this milestone can hand forward is concrete rather than aspirational — write stage artifacts incrementally so a death is resumable rather than total, order the brief by priority so a partial run still produces value, and never substitute the orchestrator for the read-only reviewer no matter what it costs in wall time.

5. **A green governance suite whose meaning depends on which laptop ran it.** E13 reported governance as **186 passed, 18 skipped**; the same suite on the SME's box reported **203 passed, 1 skipped** — same 204 total, *"seventeen tests moving between passed and skipped purely because I have the RCA reference manuals on disk and your environment does not have every `*_MANUAL_PATH` wired."* Two engineers, one commit, two different meanings of green. That is [#220](https://github.com/Siddardth7/quality-engineering-skills/issues/220) (the citation gate skips instead of failing when a manual is absent, so CI can verify zero quotations and still report green) and [#221](https://github.com/Siddardth7/quality-engineering-skills/issues/221) (wiring the on-box manual paths for E5/E6/E8) reproduced by accident rather than argued for. E12 built the one defence available inside its own scope — its `CITATIONS.tsv` subset check raises if the manifest declares no rows *and* raises if the skill body names no id, so it cannot pass by matching nothing — but that is one test inoculating itself, not a fix for the gate.
