## Overview

Milestone 7 (`v0.7.0`) delivers the **NCR & COPQ Financial Estimator** for the **Engine-Powered Quality Engineering Skills** platform. It represents the platform's **second net-new domain built from scratch** (following the RCA Suite at `v0.6.0`), implementing Category 5 from [`ROADMAP.md`](../../ROADMAP.md) (Documentation / Cost-of-Quality) across two deterministic engines:

1. **NCR — Nonconformance Reporting (ISO 9001 §8.7 & IATF 16949 §8.7)**:
   - Formulates raw shop-floor defect notes into objective-evidence nonconformance language (what deviated, requirement violated, measured evidence, quantity, detection point — zero personal blame, zero premature root-cause speculation) per ISO 9001:2015 Clause 8.7.
   - Evaluates rule-based nonconformance dispositions across five standard pathways (`Scrap`, `Rework`, `UseAsIs`, `ReturnToVendor`, `Regrade`) with cited rationale, required approval authority (Material Review Board / Customer Authorization per IATF 16949:2016 §8.7.1.1/§8.7.1.7), and FMEA risk analysis triggers.
   - Enforces a mandatory load-bearing `INSUFFICIENT_DATA` negative control on ambiguous defect data, preventing silent disposition decisions.

2. **COPQ — Cost of Poor Quality Financial Estimator (PAF Model)**:
   - Implements classical Prevention-Appraisal-Failure (PAF) cost accounting cited against the ASQ Certified Six Sigma Green Belt (CSSGB) Body of Knowledge, *The Certified Six Sigma Green Belt Handbook* (2nd Ed.), and the CSSC Lean Six Sigma Green Belt Manual (2018).
   - Computes itemized internal failure costs (scrap `scrap_qty * unit_cost`, rework labor and added material, containment/sorting `sort_hours * labor_rate`, retest, and machine downtime), itemized external failure costs (warranty claims `warranty_units * warranty_cost_per_unit`, customer returns, recall logistics, and price concessions), conformance costs (CoGQ = Prevention + Appraisal), aggregate COPQ, total Cost of Quality (CoQ = CoGQ + COPQ), %-of-revenue, and failure cost distribution ratios.
   - Enforces strict non-negative bounds, division-by-zero protection when `revenue_base` is omitted, and finite numeric validation.

Milestone 7 fully realizes the **Product Triad** architecture for both new domains:
- **Deterministic Core** (`packages/quality-core/src/quality_core/ncr/`, `packages/quality-core/src/quality_core/copq/`): Typed Pydantic schemas, validation and estimation engines, `ASSUMPTIONS_LOG.md`, and machine-checkable `CITATIONS.tsv` validated against on-machine reference manuals.
- **Visual Single-Writer Canvases** (`packages/quality-core/src/quality_core/canvas/ncr.py`, `packages/quality-core/src/quality_core/canvas/copq.py`): `NCRCanvas` (responsive HTML nonconformance card log) and `COPQCanvas` (responsive HTML PAF distribution waterfall bar and financial Pareto ranking table) with benchmark manufacturing datasets.
- **FastMCP Tool Layer** (`packages/quality-mcp/src/quality_mcp/tools/ncr.py`, `packages/quality-mcp/src/quality_mcp/tools/copq.py`, `packages/quality-mcp/src/quality_mcp/tools/canvas.py`): Exposes five FastMCP tools (`write_ncr`, `recommend_disposition`, `render_ncr_canvas`, `estimate_copq`, and `render_copq_canvas`).
- **AI Agent Skills** (`skills/ncr-writing/`, `skills/copq-estimator/`): `agentskills.io` compliant domain skills enforcing the Zero Inline Math / Zero Inline Adjudication invariant — all disposition routing is delegated to `recommend_disposition` and all financial arithmetic is routed through `estimate_copq`.
- **Unified Client Round-Trip & Chained Workflow**: In-process JSON-RPC integration suite (`test_ncr_copq_client_roundtrip.py`) validating dual payload parity, session error isolation, and chained multi-tool workflows (`write_ncr` $\rightarrow$ `recommend_disposition` $\rightarrow$ `estimate_copq` $\rightarrow$ `render_copq_canvas`) in a single FastMCP client session.
